# Product

## Platform
web
## Purpose and confirmed scope
Modernize Juan Manuel Ramírez Pérez's existing writer website. Preserve Inicio, Biografía, Libros, Blog, free book downloads and subscription access. Source: https://juanmanuelramirezescritor.com/.
## Confirmed visual commitment
Option A approved by user, adapted to blue ink; orange must remain available as an interchangeable palette. Hero is intended as a video of the author writing, reading and planning in his study. The user-supplied video is installed at `dist/assets/juan-manuel-escribiendo.mp4` and enabled in the hero, with muted autoplay, looping, pause/resume and reduced-motion support. The original illustrative study poster remains the fallback; the supplied video is not presented as evidence of authentic filming.
## Assets and evidence
Nine original covers, eight original PDF links, original author portrait, and original Nerdopolitan interview video. Sonetos has no separate PDF linked in the original download section. No invented quotations, awards or publication dates.
## Stack
Static multipage HTML/CSS/JavaScript, selected for this content-led site under the Sites workflow. No subscription provider credentials supplied: preserve access to the original subscription form rather than claiming to store emails.
